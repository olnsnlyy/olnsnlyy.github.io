I am a researcher in the Department of Radiology at Asan Medical Center, Seoul. My research focuses on deep learning-based quantitative brain MRI biomarkers and their application to clinical patient cohorts, working closely with neuroradiologists and neurologists.

My work spans automated lesion detection and segmentation with nnU-Net (e.g., paramagnetic rim lesions on QSM in multiple sclerosis, choroid plexus segmentation), glymphatic imaging markers (DTI-ALPS, perivascular spaces, gadolinium T1 mapping) in ALS, long COVID, MS, Parkinson's disease and normal aging, and oxygen metabolic mapping (OEF, CMRO2) from DSC perfusion and QSM–qBOLD.

I received my M.S. in Medical Science (Biomedical Engineering) from the University of Ulsan College of Medicine, Asan Medical Center, advised by Seung Chai Jung, M.D., Ph.D., with a thesis on proton-density MR angiography for intracranial aneurysm detection. I received my B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies. I have contributed to 15 peer-reviewed publications (2025–2026).

Feel free to reach out about quantitative MRI, neuroimaging biomarkers, or medical image segmentation.
