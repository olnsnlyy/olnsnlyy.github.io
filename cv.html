Research Projects

Mar 2026 – Present
Oxygen Metabolic Characterization of Paramagnetic Rim Lesions in MS

Applied a deep learning QSM–qBOLD model to multi-echo GRE in 110 MS patients (391 lesions) and 35 healthy controls, mapping OEF, susceptibility, R2, and venous blood volume across lesion zones and relating lesion-level patterns to clinical disability (EDSS). First-author manuscript under review.

Dec 2025 – Present
Structure–Function Coupling Across Clinical Stages of ALS

Measured structure–function coupling in 77 ALS patients (King's stages 1–4) and 73 healthy controls, showing stage-dependent coupling decline in global, cingulo-opercular, and default-mode networks. First-author manuscript under review.

Apr 2025 – Present
Proton-Density MR Angiography for Intracranial Aneurysm Detection (M.S. Thesis)

Developed an automated volume-rendering pipeline for proton-density MR angiography using TOF-guided vessel labeling and 3D nnU-Net segmentation (Dice 0.87). Aneurysm detection was evaluated against TOF-MRA by two independent neuroradiologists, showing comparable diagnostic performance. First-author manuscript in preparation.
